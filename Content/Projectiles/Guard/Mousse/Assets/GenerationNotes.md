Follow-up after the player reported that the signature light was still hard to see in game: the prior offscreen image did contain the column, but its visible body was only a thin near-white line on a bright background. The held column now uses a 37-pixel-wide tinted NonPremultiplied body and separate 27/12-pixel Additive highlights, sized by the measured mask bounds; the outgoing crescent also has a colored body and restrained Additive core. A second FNA offscreen rendering on light and dark backgrounds showed the colored body in both facings and both weapon palettes. This is still not an in-game visual acceptance test.

Slash Dust now uses 30 evenly spaced radial launch angles with at most 0.08 radian angular jitter, a bounded 4–20 world-pixel origin radius and 3.5–8 world-pixel initial speed before weapon scale. Each dust stores its own spin rate in customData; Astesia's custom shards likewise get individual angular velocities. Existing hit-feedback particles remain tied to hit positions.

These passes use nonzero draw alpha and LinearClamp, then restore the world AlphaBlend batch. Earlier alpha-zero draw comments below are superseded.
